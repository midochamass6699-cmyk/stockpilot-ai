A practical checklist for researching a U.S. stock

  • Confirm the ticker and the exchange where the security trades.
  • Check the quote timestamp, trading volume and whether the market is open.
  • Read company filings and earnings information; understand debt, cash flow and dilution.
  • Compare recent news with the company's own disclosures.
  • Consider volatility, liquidity, fees and the possibility of losing money—especially with lower-priced stocks.

U.S. market hours

The regular NYSE and Nasdaq trading session is generally 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time on U.S. trading days. Holidays, early closes and daylight-saving changes can affect schedules. Always verify the current exchange calendar.

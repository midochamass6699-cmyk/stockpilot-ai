How the StockPilot priority-fit score works

The score is an editorial comparison against the priorities listed below. It is not a regulator rating, a guarantee of service quality, or a recommendation to buy securities or open an account. Scores are approximate and can change as fees and policies change.

30%Trading costs and commission structure
25%Phone support and review footprint
20%Withdrawal speed and policy clarity
15%Ease of navigating the platform
5%Web, desktop and mobile access
5%Clarity of account application process

Important distinction: a zero-commission headline does not mean every trade is free. Currency conversion, exchange/regulatory charges, options fees, market data, spreads and other account fees can apply.

Reviews: Trustpilot scores and counts are snapshots of public profiles, often global rather than Canada-only or product-specific. The volume or rating of reviews does not establish whether a broker is safe, solvent or suitable.

Eligibility, identity checks and withdrawal holds: Brokerage accounts are subject to legal age, residency, identity verification and other eligibility rules. Approval is never guaranteed to be instant. Do not choose a platform because it promises weaker checks. Brokers may delay or restrict withdrawals for unsettled trades, recent deposits, transfers, compliance reviews or fraud prevention. No broker can responsibly promise “no holds” in every circumstance. This page is general educational information, not financial advice or account-opening guidance.
Check registration first: Canadian investors can check firms through the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO) and the Canadian Securities Administrators. U.S. firms can be checked through FINRA BrokerCheck. Check the exact legal entity serving your jurisdiction.

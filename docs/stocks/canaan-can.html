Company overview

Canaan designs computing equipment used in cryptocurrency mining and related applications. Its business is exposed to hardware demand, mining economics, manufacturing costs and crypto-market cycles.

Business metrics to understand

Hardware shipments, mining-equipment demand, inventory, manufacturing costs, cash position and exposure to crypto-market cycles.

Research lens: what to examine beyond the share price

This profile is a starting point, not a complete valuation. These company-specific questions help readers review primary disclosures and test a business narrative. They are not conclusions about current performance.

  • For mining hardware, examine shipment volumes, average selling prices, product efficiency and gross margin across different demand cycles.
  • Separate equipment sales from any self-mining activity. Consider bitcoin price, network difficulty, energy costs and the capital tied up in mining operations.
  • Review inventory, receivables, cash, financing, geographic exposure and the volatility of customer demand.

Evidence to consult: the latest annual and quarterly filings, earnings release, investor presentation and risk-factor disclosures. For U.S.-listed issuers, use SEC EDGAR. Check reporting periods before comparing figures.

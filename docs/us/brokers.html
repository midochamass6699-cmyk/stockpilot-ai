What to compare before evaluating a brokerage

  • Account types and eligibility, including any minimum-age and residency requirements.
  • Trading commissions, regulatory charges, account fees and transfer costs.
  • Cash-sweep arrangements, interest rates and how uninvested cash is treated.
  • Margin interest, borrowing terms and the firm's right to liquidate collateral.
  • Available markets, securities, research tools, data subscriptions and customer support.
  • How the firm is registered and what investor-protection arrangements apply.

U.S. investor verification resources

  • FINRA BrokerCheck — research brokerage firms and registered individuals.
  • SEC Investor.gov — investor education and registration checks.
  • SEC EDGAR — public-company filings.
  • SIPC — information about the limits of securities-account protection. SIPC does not protect against market losses.

Always read current disclosures and account agreements. Product availability and regulatory status can change.

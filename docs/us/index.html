STOCK MARKET RESEARCH · QUICK ANSWERS

U.S. Stock Market Research: Stocks, Penny Stocks & Crypto FAQs

What does StockPilot AI show?

StockPilot AI provides a market overview with selected stocks to watch, a penny-stock screen and leading cryptocurrencies ranked by reported 24-hour trading volume. The lists summarize activity in the available data feeds and are starting points for independent research.

How are the top stocks selected?

The U.S. candidate set is ranked using a 0–100 market activity score: 35% relative volume, 30% absolute daily price movement, 20% absolute 5-day movement, 10% absolute 20-day movement and 5% average dollar liquidity, with a modest penalty for unusually high volatility. Both gainers and decliners can qualify. Main stocks must have at least US$1 million in 20-day average daily dollar volume; penny stocks must be priced US$0.05–US$10 and have at least US$100,000 in average daily dollar volume. The feed is limited to available public screeners and is not an exhaustive scan of every U.S. listing.

What does StockPilot AI mean by penny stocks?

For this site's penny-stock screen, qualifying shares are priced from US$0.10 to US$10 when data is available. Definitions vary by market, exchange and data provider. Lower-priced shares can be volatile and carry substantial risk.

How are the top cryptocurrencies ranked?

The crypto section ranks available coins by reported 24-hour trading volume in US dollars. Prices and trading volume can change quickly, and the displayed list is limited to assets available through the CoinGecko feed.

Where can I compare online brokers?

Visit our U.S. brokerage directory for a starting list of firms and research the current account terms, fees and eligibility directly with each provider.

Is StockPilot AI financial advice?

No. StockPilot AI provides general market information for educational and research purposes. The lists are not personalized investment advice or recommendations to buy or sell securities. Verify information with primary sources and consider your own circumstances and risk tolerance.

HOW THE DAILY LISTS WORK

How StockPilot AI Selects Market Picks

The U.S. edition filters the published stock lists to records marked with market="US". The candidate universe is drawn from Yahoo Finance public screeners for penny stocks, most-active shares, daily gainers and small-cap gainers. The data file is a limited candidate scan, not a complete inventory of every U.S. listing.

The published momentum score ranges from 0 to 100. Its stated weighting is 10% 5-day return, 30% 20-day return, 25% 50-day return, 20% relative volume and 15% trend alignment, with deductions for elevated RSI and volatility. The current screen also requires positive 20-day momentum and a price above the 20-day average. Main-stock candidates are priced above US$10; penny-stock candidates range from US$0.10 to US$10 when qualifying data is available.

Stock prices are shown in U.S. dollars for U.S.-market records. The stock data file uses Yahoo Finance public screeners and daily historical prices through yfinance; those unofficial data endpoints may be throttled or changed. Cryptocurrency market data is supplied by CoinGecko and ranked by reported 24-hour trading volume in USD.

Provider coverage, reporting times and data availability can affect the lists. Canadian-market records are excluded from this U.S. edition. Quote dates are shown on individual stock cards; data may be delayed and the feed is not an exhaustive ranking of every U.S.-listed security.

Trading volume measures market activity, not company quality, future performance or suitability for an individual investor. Always verify current information and do your own research.

A Different Perspective on Investing

Recent events surrounding NVIDIA remind us that no company, regardless of its size or reputation, is immune to unexpected developments. My personal interest has always been in smaller companies and penny stocks. I believe that careful research, understanding a company’s fundamentals and recognizing emerging opportunities are essential when evaluating these investments.

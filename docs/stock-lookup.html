How to look up a stock

Enter a company's name or ticker symbol, then select Search stocks. When multiple listings appear, compare the company name, ticker, and exchange before selecting one.

The result displays the listing name, ticker, exchange, currency, latest available price, and the quote time supplied by the data provider.

U.S. and Canadian stock symbols

A ticker can represent different listings on different exchanges. Canadian listings may use exchange-specific suffixes, while U.S. listings commonly use the ticker alone.

If a search returns several matches, use the exchange information to identify the listing you intended to research.

How to interpret a quote result

A quote is a snapshot of a market data feed, not a complete description of a company. Before comparing two prices, confirm that the listings use the same currency, exchange and share class. A U.S. quote in dollars and a Canadian quote in Canadian dollars are not directly comparable without accounting for currency and listing differences.

Check the timestamp and market session. A displayed price may represent the last reported trade rather than a live executable price. Outside regular market hours, during trading halts or when a provider has incomplete coverage, the latest available value may be old or absent.

Price is not valuation

Share price alone does not tell you whether a company is large, profitable or inexpensive. To understand scale, examine market capitalization and the number of shares outstanding. To understand financial condition, review cash, debt, cash flow and the company's latest filings.

When a search returns several matches

Do not select the first result automatically. Match the issuer name and exchange, check the trading currency, and confirm whether the security is a common share, depositary receipt or another instrument. If the listing identity is unclear, pause and consult the exchange or issuer's investor-relations page.

Data limitations: this lookup displays information returned by its configured provider. It does not independently verify every exchange, guarantee complete symbol coverage, or replace an exchange's official quote service.

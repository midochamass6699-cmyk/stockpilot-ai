Where Should You Invest?

Understand some of the main investment choices available to investors.

Individual Stocks

A stock represents ownership in a company. Its market value can rise or fall as the business performs and investor expectations change.

ETFs

Exchange-traded funds can hold many investments in one product. They can provide diversification, but still carry market risk and fees.

Bonds and Fixed Income

Bonds represent money lent to a government or company. Understand interest rates, maturity dates, credit risk, and potential changes in value.

Canadian Investment Accounts

Learn about the TFSA, RRSP, and FHSA. Each account has different eligibility requirements, tax treatment, and contribution limits.

How to Start Investing

A simple starting guide for people who are new to investing.

01 — Define Your Goals Decide why you want to invest and when you may need your money.
02 — Understand Your Risk Learn about volatility, possible losses, and your risk tolerance.
03 — Research Your Investments Compare companies, ETFs, fees, diversification, and risks.
04 — Compare Brokerages Look at account choices, fees, available investments, and regulatory protections.
05 — Start Carefully Avoid investing money you need for immediate expenses. Never assume profits are guaranteed.
06 — Keep Learning Review your investments and continue developing your understanding of the market.

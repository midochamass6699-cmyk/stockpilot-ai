Build Your Investing Knowledge

Start with the fundamentals

What Is a Stock?

A stock represents a share of ownership in a company. When you buy shares, you participate in the company's potential gains and losses.

  • Stock prices can rise or fall.
  • Some companies pay dividends.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Where Can You Invest?

Canadians can research brokerage platforms and investment accounts before deciding where to invest.

  • Compare fees and trading costs.
  • Understand TFSA and RRSP account rules.
  • Check that a firm is properly registered.

How to Research a Company

Look beyond the ticker symbol. Understand the business.

  • Read quarterly and annual financial reports.
  • Review revenue, earnings, and debt.
  • Understand the company's industry and competitors.

Understand Risk

Investing always involves uncertainty. A company can perform differently from what investors expect.

  • Avoid investing money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Consider diversification.
  • Be cautious about hype and promises of quick profits.

Three Habits of Better Investors

01 · Do Your Research

Understand what a company sells, how it earns money, and what could affect its future.

02 · Think Long Term

Look beyond daily price movements. Consider business performance and your own time horizon.

03 · Keep Learning

Follow credible information, question assumptions, and learn from both gains and losses.

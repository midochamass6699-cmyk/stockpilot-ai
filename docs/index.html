Why StockPilot?

StockPilot started with a simple idea: investing knowledge should not be reserved for people who can afford expensive services or memberships.

I started with very little and wanted to learn how to understand the market independently. StockPilot is a place to share research resources and make investing education more accessible.

This is an independent learning and research project. We believe in learning, asking questions and making informed decisions.

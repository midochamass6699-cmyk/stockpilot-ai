HOW THE DAILY LISTS WORK

How StockPilot AI Selects Market Picks

StockPilot AI highlights market activity rather than promising investment results. The main stock list and penny-stock list are ranked by reported trading volume within the available actively traded stock-data feed. Main-stock candidates are priced above US$10; penny-stock candidates are priced from US$0.10 to US$10, when qualifying data is available.

The cryptocurrency list ranks the available coins by reported 24-hour trading volume in US dollars. Stock information is supplied by Alpha Vantage and cryptocurrency market information is supplied by CoinGecko. Provider coverage, reporting times and data availability can affect the lists; the stock feed is not an exhaustive ranking of every listed security.

Trading volume measures market activity, not company quality, future performance or suitability for an individual investor. Always verify current information and do your own research before making financial decisions.

THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

Why I Started StockPilot AI

I created StockPilot AI with one simple idea: make daily stock-market research easier to follow.

The market moves quickly, and finding useful information can take time. I wanted to build a straightforward place where people can explore actively traded stocks, discover penny stocks and review key market information without unnecessary clutter.

StockPilot AI is an independent project built to make market information easier to access. It is a starting point for research—not a substitute for your own decisions.
